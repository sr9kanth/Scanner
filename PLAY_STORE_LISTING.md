## Full Description

Is your phone showing strange pop-ups? Getting too many notifications? Worried about suspicious apps?

**CleanGuard AI** is your personal AI phone health assistant — designed especially for parents, grandparents, and anyone who wants a safer, cleaner phone without needing to be a tech expert.

✨ **What CleanGuard AI does:**

🔍 **Smart App Scanner**
Scans all your installed apps and gives each one a safety score. We explain what we find in plain, friendly language — no tech jargon.

🤖 **Dual AI Analysis**
Powered by Google Gemini AI and DeepSeek AI working together. When both AIs agree, you can be confident in the result.

📸 **Screenshot Analysis**
See a suspicious pop-up or scary message? Just take a screenshot and let our AI tell you if it’s real or a scam.

🔔 **Notification Monitor**
Tracks which apps are sending you too many notifications and makes it easy to turn them off.

🔒 **Accessibility & Pop-up Protection**
Finds apps that may be watching your screen or showing fake virus alerts.

🌐 **Chrome Cleanup Wizard**
Step-by-step guide to remove browser popups, fake notification subscriptions, and suspicious websites from Chrome.

📊 **Phone Health Score**
Get an overall score (0–100) for your phone’s health, updated with every scan.

👴👵 **Grandparent Mode**
Extra large text, one step at a time, and the simplest possible explanations.

**Your privacy is our priority:**
✔️ No personal data uploaded
✔️ No APK files transmitted (only anonymous hashes for virus checking)
✔️ No contacts, SMS, or call logs accessed
✔️ Works in offline mode

*CleanGuard AI is a phone health and privacy assistant. It is not a certified antivirus product.*
