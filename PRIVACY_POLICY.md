# Privacy Policy — CleanGuard AI

**Last updated: June 2025**

## Overview

CleanGuard AI ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## What We Collect

### Data Analyzed Locally (Never Uploaded)
- List of installed app names and package names
- App permission lists
- Notification frequency statistics
- Accessibility service states
- Overlay permission states

### Data Sent to Third-Party AI Services
When you use AI analysis features, we send:
- **Technical metadata only**: app name, risk score, permission list, category
- **Screenshot images**: only when you explicitly choose to upload a screenshot for analysis
- **No personal data**: we never send contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, or any personal content

### Data Sent to VirusTotal
- **SHA-256 hashes only**: We hash app files and submit the hash — we never upload the actual APK file

## Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Google Gemini API | AI threat analysis | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| DeepSeek API | AI validation | https://www.deepseek.com/privacy |
| VirusTotal | Malware hash lookup | https://support.virustotal.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002168385 |

## Data Storage

All scan results, app data, and notification statistics are stored locally on your device using an encrypted Room database. We do not operate servers that store your data.

## Data Retention

Scan results are retained for 30 days and then automatically deleted. You can delete all data at any time from Settings.

## Children's Privacy

CleanGuard AI does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Your Rights

- **Access**: View all stored data in the app's Report screen
- **Deletion**: Delete all data from Settings > Privacy > Clear All Data
- **Opt-out**: Disable AI analysis features to prevent any data from leaving your device

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@cleanguard.ai
