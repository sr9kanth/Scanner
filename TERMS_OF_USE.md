## Description of Service

CleanGuard AI is an AI-powered phone health checkup tool. It helps identify potentially risky apps, excessive permissions, and browser-related issues. It is **not** a certified antivirus product and should not be relied upon as the sole security measure for your device.

## What CleanGuard AI Does NOT Do

- CleanGuard AI **never** automatically uninstalls apps
- CleanGuard AI **never** modifies your browser data without your consent
- CleanGuard AI **never** changes Android settings without your explicit action
- CleanGuard AI **never** uploads your personal files, photos, contacts, or messages

## Limitations

- AI threat assessments are probabilistic and may produce false positives or false negatives
- CleanGuard AI is not a substitute for professional security advice
- Risk scores are based on behavioral signals and may not reflect the actual safety of an app
- VirusTotal results represent community-sourced data and may be incomplete
